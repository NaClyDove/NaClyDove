## 👋 About Me

I'm Frederik, an AI engineer from Denmark 🇩🇰.

By day I'm a **Senior AI Engineer at [Abion](https://abion.com)**, where I build AI automation for **brand protection**. That means finding lookalike domains, catching infringements and phishing before they catch you, and turning slow manual review work into agents that do the heavy lifting.

Before this I founded **Omika AI** and **Ace League** (esports), where I got deep into multi-agent systems, long-running agents, and shipping AI to production.
